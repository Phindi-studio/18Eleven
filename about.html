About 18ELEVEN FC

A community-driven youth football club, established in 2026 in Mamelodi, South Africa.

Home / About
Background

Our Story

18ELEVEN FC was established in 2022 with the goal of giving young players from disadvantaged communities an opportunity to develop their football skills, build confidence and grow through sport.

Since its establishment, the team has provided accessible football training and opportunities for young players to participate in local tournaments, giving them valuable experience both on and off the field.

Families contribute R50 per month towards soccer equipment and programme costs. However, no child is turned away from training because their family is unable to contribute. We believe every child should have the opportunity to develop their talent and enjoy the benefits of football, regardless of their financial circumstances.

Every child deserves the chance to play — regardless of financial circumstances.
What Drives Us

Mission & Goal

Our Mission

Mission

To provide young players with a safe and supportive environment where they can develop their football skills, discipline, confidence and teamwork while enjoying the game.

Our Goal

Goal

To become a leading youth football team in the local community by developing talented young players, creating opportunities for them to progress and making a positive impact through football.

What We Stand For

Our Values

The principles every 18ELEVEN FC player, coach and parent lives by — on and off the pitch.

Discipline

We arrive on time, follow team rules and take our training and development seriously.

Teamwork

We support and encourage one another, both on and off the field, knowing success comes from working together.

Respect

We respect our teammates, coaches, opponents and referees, regardless of the outcome of the game.

Resilience

We never give up. We learn from challenges, stay determined and continue working towards our goals.

Leadership

Growing Beyond The Game

Player Development

18ELEVEN FC is committed to developing players both on and off the field — building skilled, disciplined and confident young people, not just footballers.

Football Skills

Structured training sessions that build technique, ball control, tactical awareness and match fitness.

Discipline

Consistent structure and expectations that help players build good habits on and off the pitch.

Confidence

A supportive environment that encourages every player to believe in their own ability and voice.

Teamwork

Players learn to communicate, cooperate and rely on one another to achieve shared goals.

Leadership

Older and more experienced players are encouraged to mentor and guide newer teammates.

Competitive Experience

Opportunities to apply skills and teamwork in local tournaments and organised matches.
