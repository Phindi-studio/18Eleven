Who We Are

A Club Built On Community

18ELEVEN FC was established in 2026 with the goal of giving young players from disadvantaged communities an opportunity to develop their football skills, build confidence and grow through sport.

Since its establishment, the team has provided accessible football training and opportunities for young players to participate in local tournaments, giving them valuable experience both on and off the field.

No child is turned away from training because their family is unable to contribute.
